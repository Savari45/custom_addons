Available Graph Types

Our dashboard supports multiple graph types to cater to different data visualization needs. Choose the one that fits your data and gain clearer insights with visual appeal.

  • Card: Display concise data in a compact, elegant card format, perfect for highlighting key metrics.
  • List: Organize your data in a clean and easy-to-read list format, making it ideal for sequential information.
  • Bar: Use bar charts for comparing different categories, ideal for visualizing trends and differences.
  • Line: Represent continuous data or trends over time with line charts that make fluctuations easy to identify.
  • Area: Similar to line charts, but with areas beneath the lines filled, emphasizing the magnitude of data change.
  • Pie: Illustrate proportions in data with a pie chart, offering a clear view of how different categories compare to the whole.
  • Half Pie: A semi-circular version of the pie chart, great for showing parts of a whole with emphasis.
  • Doughnut: A variation of the pie chart with a hollow center, offering a more modern, stylish look.
  • Half Doughnut: A visually compact half-doughnut chart that presents data succinctly while maximizing space.
  • Funnel: Perfect for tracking stages in a process or sales funnel, highlighting conversion rates at each step.
